Read the information below and answer Questions 8-14.

A

Wild Rose

(Tuesday 49.00)

This TV drama is about a young private
detective employed by a team of New York
businessmen who send her to Brazil to look into
a series of hotel robberies. When she gets
there, she discovers that the hotels, which are
owned by the businessmen, have been empty
for the last two years and the local authorities
have no record of any robberies.

B

Animal Planet

(Wednesday 23.00)

This is a classic black-and-white film from the
forties in which astronaut Charlie Huston crash-
lands on a planet ruled entirely by animals. It is
a first-class suspense adventure which also
looks at the human condition, although this is
not always a successful part of the film.

C

Strange Encounter

(Saturday 21.00)

Suspense is skilfully built up in this clever, small-
scale supernatural story. A young couple view a
deserted old house that they are interested in
buying, They meet a strange old lady who tells
them of the mystical powers of the house and
how previous owners have been able to travel
back through the centuries to meet their
ancestors.

(Sunday 22.00)

Paul Theroux’s account of his recent jaurney
